# CAMPAIGN V.2

## Stage 1 - Tangible Object

Start grounded.

"This is a sticker project."

Physical. Small. Real.

Backer feels:

- Low risk.
- Funny.
- Understandable.

## Stage 2 - Cultural Disruption

Then:

"These are not decorations.  
They are interventions."

Now it becomes commentary.

Backer feels:

- Ah. This is critique.

Still safe.

## Stage 3 - Public Scale

Introduce stretch goals that affect space around people.

- Wall-size sticker.
- Public installation.
- Pop-up useless office.

Now scale increases physically.

Backer feels:

- This could become visible.

## Stage 4 - Monumental Absurdity

- Island sculpture.
- Floating useless billboard in ocean.
- Permanent "Nothing Happens Here" monument.

Now absurdity is architectural.

Backer feels:

- This is art.

But you never say it.

## Stage 5 - Cosmic Insignificance

Only after all that:

Space mission.

By now, it feels like logical escalation.

Small sticker -> bigger sticker -> building scale -> planetary scale.

The space mission becomes the ultimate useless gesture.

Psychologically satisfying.
