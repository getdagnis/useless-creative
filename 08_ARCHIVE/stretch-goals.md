## 3️⃣ Artistically Absurd Stretch Goals

The key:

- They must sound feasible.
- But emotionally wrong.

- **€5,000**  
  10 new sticker designs unlocked.

- **€8,000**  
  Metallic useless sticker added to all eligible tiers.

- **€12,000**  
  Glow-in-the-dark confusion sticker unlocked.

- **€20,000**  
  Completely Useless Poster series unlocked.

- **€30,000**  
  First run of Completely Useless Objects.

- **€100,000**  
  We produce an oversized “Collector Edition” sticker sheet no one can place cleanly on anything.

- **€500,000**  
  We purchase an abandoned billboard in the desert and display a completely blank advertisement for one year.

- **€2,000,000**  
  We build a public staircase that ascends into the air and stops.

- **€10,000,000**  
  We commission a 60-meter concrete hand-gesture sculpture on an uninhabited Pacific island.  
  No signage. No explanation.  
  Visible only from satellite imagery.

- **€50,000,000**  
  We organize a private aerospace mission to place a `#WEAREUSELESS` sticker on the side of a vehicle currently orbiting the sun.  
  Color preference: red.
